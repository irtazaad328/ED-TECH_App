# Week 4 – App Demo Video Script (2–3 min)

**Owner:** Idiris Hassan Mohamed · **Branch:** `feature/week4-idiris-demo-and-details`

## Before you record

- Run on a phone-sized device or emulator (e.g. Pixel 7). If using Chrome, set DevTools device mode to 390×844.
- Do a **hot restart** right before recording so the cart and enrolments start empty.
- Recorder: Windows **Win + Alt + R** (Xbox Game Bar), or OBS. 1080p, 30 fps, mic on.
- Close notifications and keep the cursor still between taps.

## Shot list

| Time | Screen | Action on screen | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | Welcome | Show logo, tap **Get Started** | "This is Ed Tech by Team 6, a learning app for learners and teachers." |
| 0:10–0:25 | Role Selection | Pause on both cards, tap **Learner** | "First you choose a role. The app has separate Learner and Teacher flows." |
| 0:25–0:50 | Learner Login | Tap **Login** with fields empty to show the errors. Type a bad email to show the format error, then valid details and log in | "The login form checks input as you go: required fields, email format and password rules." |
| 0:50–1:05 | Learner Home | Scroll the portal slowly, then open **Programs** | "The Home Portal is the learner's dashboard." |
| 1:05–1:30 | Programs List | Type "Flutter" in search, clear it. Tap the **AI** and **UI/UX** category chips, then **All** | "Programs load from JSON. You can search by name or filter by category." |
| 1:30–2:00 | Program Details | Open **Flutter Mobile App Development**. Scroll to **Syllabus**. Open Module 1 (point at the free *Preview* lesson and the locks), then open Module 2 and show Module 1 closing | "The details page shows the course info and an expandable syllabus. Only one module opens at a time, and lessons stay locked until you enrol." |
| 2:00–2:20 | Cart → Payment | Scroll back up, tap **Add to Cart**. Show the cart, tap checkout, pay with the pre-filled card | "Add to Cart sends the course to the cart, and checkout goes through the payment form." |
| 2:20–2:35 | Details (Enrolled) | Tap **Go to Home Portal** → Programs → Flutter. Show the green **Enrolled** badge, the unlocked lessons and **Continue Learning** | "After payment the course shows as Enrolled, every lesson is unlocked, and the button changes to Continue Learning." |
| 2:35–2:55 | Feedback Form | Fill in a rating and comment, submit, show the confirmation | "Finally, learners can send feedback on a program." |
| 2:55–3:00 | — | End on the Home Portal | "That's Ed Tech. Thanks for watching." |

> ⚠️ **Feedback Form:** there's no feedback screen in the codebase yet (checked on 1 Nov). Record that segment once the teammate who owns it has merged it, or cut the segment and finish at 2:35.
